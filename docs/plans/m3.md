## Goal
`Sketch::drag` returns a `DragSession` whose every `to` is one frame: the
target held as a `Goal` the required constraints can never be pushed off
by, solved from the previous step with least change, its branch observed
and compared, a flipping step bisected. Fixture 4 (`slotted-plate-drag`)
passes in full under both algorithms: every step's coordinates as the
roadmap lists them, checker green, DoF 3, no flip, `Q`'s bits unchanged,
each step's digest equal natively and on wasm32-wasip1, each step ≤ 1 ms.
ADR-0006 closes branch preservation, the last kickoff `⚠ OPEN:`.

## Design deltas
- **Priority** (ARCHITECTURE.md §Solve, §Priorities): `system::Equation`
  gains a priority, `Required | Goal`; goals are compiled after every
  required equation and join the component of the parameters they read.
  The driver solves lexicographically: each iteration takes the required
  step as today (LM or DogLeg, minimum norm), then the goal's least-squares
  step restricted to the null space of the required Jacobian (the basis
  from ADR-0003's QR), minimum norm within it, and corrects back onto the
  required equations. A component with goals converges when the required
  equations meet acceptance and the goal is met or stationary in that null
  space (`|Nᵀ J_gᵀ g|` under a named `GOAL_STATIONARY`, measured by step 1).
  An unreachable target (fixture 4's y) is converged, not `NotConverged`.
  Diagnostics read only required equations: DoF, redundancy and conflicts
  never see a goal.
  *Settled (step 1)*: goals are `System::goals`, a list of their own
  compiled after `equations`, not a priority field on `Equation`: a goal
  has no constraint source, and keeping it out of `equations` keeps every
  diagnostic blind to it by construction. The goal step is reduced
  Levenberg–Marquardt with the required curvature (SQP: `H = (J_g N)ᵀ(J_g
  N) + Nᵀ W N`, `W` by a forward difference of the analytic Jacobian),
  cut to one component size, accepted on a decrease beyond the
  correction's noise: the Gauss–Newton step alone, with or without a line
  search, spent the whole budget on a point reaching inside its circle and
  on a linkage past its span (ARCHITECTURE.md §Priorities). Stationary is
  `|Nᵀ J_gᵀ g|` within the polishing acceptance plus `GOAL_ROUNDING =
  1e-12 · |g|`, not a pure ratio: a ratio test stalls at √eps, where the
  objective's decrease rounds away.
- **Branches** (ARCHITECTURE.md §Branches; ADR-0006, step 2): an
  `Observation` per arc (its sweep, tracked across solves), per `parallel`
  (sign of `dot`), per `angle` (sign of `cos(φ − θ)`), per circle (sign of
  its radius). A sign is *known* only beyond a named margin; a quantity
  inside it on either side is not compared. An arc flips when its tracked
  sweep leaves (0, 2π), not when `cross(s − c, e − c)` changes sign (the
  slot's semicircles sit at sweep π, where that sign is rounding). The ADR
  also decides what a still-flipping step returns.
  *Settled (step 2)*: `perpendicular` is observed too (sign of `cross`):
  its ±90° roots are the same kind as `parallel`'s, and leaving it out
  would let a drag swap them unnamed. The margin is no new constant: an
  entity's length tolerance at the observed coordinates (a branch is known
  exactly when the entity is not degenerate) and `tolerance::ANGLE` for a
  line pair. Flip `was`/`now` are the observed quantities (sweep, radius,
  cosine). Observations live in the internal `branch` module
  (`Observations::at`/`next`/`flips`).
- **Public types** (`laman::solution`, `laman::drag`): `Diagnostics.flips:
  Vec<Flip>`, `Flip { subject: FlipSubject (constraint or entity ref), was,
  now }`; a static solve compares the solution with the guesses.
  `SolverOptions::max_bisections` (default measured by step 5).
  `Sketch::drag(&self, point: PointId, &SolverOptions) -> Result<DragSession,
  ModelError>`; `DragSession::to(&mut self, [f64; 2]) -> Solution`,
  `finish(self) -> Solution`. The session owns a clone of the sketch and
  the last solution, compiled per step; `finish`'s solution applies to the
  unedited sketch. Refusals: `WrongKind` for a non-point, a new
  `ModelError::FixedPoint { id }` for ground; a point with one fixed
  coordinate drags along the other. `to` always returns a `Solution`: a
  non-finite target keeps the last solution's coordinates and reports
  `NotConverged` (step 4 tests it).
- **Serde** (DATA-MODEL.md §The serde form): `Solution`'s `diagnostics`
  gains `flips`, absent when empty: an optional field, no version bump.
  Fixtures' `expected.json` gains optional `flips` (default empty).
- **Digest**: a drag step's digest is `of_solution` over the step's
  solution, prefixed by the step's index and target bits. Adding `flips`
  to the solution digest re-blesses the corpus once, in step 3, saying so.
- **Glossary**: *Observation* (what no equation holds, recorded per
  solution), *Bisection* (halving a flipping drag step toward the last
  good target); *Goal* sharpened to "lexicographic, in the null space".

## Steps
Grades are tiers (`.agents/tiers.md`): **[1]** routine — the design says
exactly what to write and the tests are mechanical; **[2]** careful — a
numeric or geometric case to get right within a given design; **[3]**
unproven — an algorithm whose robustness, convergence or bound has to be
established here.

- [x] Step 1 **[3]** — Goal priority in `system` and the driver
  (internal): the lexicographic step under both engines. Tests in
  `solve::tests`: fixture 4's ten targets reach the roadmap's coordinates,
  `Q`'s bits unchanged; an unreachable goal converges stationary; property:
  a goal never leaves a required residual over acceptance. Measures
  `GOAL_ROUNDING` and the per-step iteration counts into ARCHITECTURE.md.
- [x] Step 2 **[3]** — ADR-0006 (branch preservation) and the internal

  π) at rounding noise, not flipping.

## Open questions
- `⚠ OPEN:` whether each drag step runs the full diagnostics (redundancy
  recompiles per constraint) or a frame-cheap subset with the rest on
  `finish`. Agent, by step 8, from the measured cost; full by default.
- *Settled (step 2, ADR-0006)*: the margin is the degeneracy length
  tolerance and `ANGLE`; inside it the last known branch is carried, so a
  crossing over two frames is still a flip, and a branch first known after
  an unknown start is adopted without one.
- *Settled (step 2, ADR-0006)*: a step that still flips after
  `max_bisections` returns the solution at the farthest target that kept
  every branch (the last solution if none did), the flip named; the
  session stays on its branch.
