## Goal

The three static C1 fixtures and their redundant and conflicting twins solve
from their guesses with the roadmap's coordinates, statuses, geometric DoF
and named constraints. Geometric DoF covers regular configurations and C1's
tangent contacts; the solver returns finite best-effort coordinates within
hard budgets and preserves coordinate bits on an already satisfied sketch.

## Non-goals

General singular-sketch rigidity; drag, Goal priority and branch preservation
(M3); rigid-cluster decomposition (C2); Python solver bindings (M4).
Existing Python API compatibility remains required.

## Design deltas

- ARCHITECTURE.md §Solve and DATA-MODEL.md §Diagnostics: geometric DoF replaces
  raw Jacobian nullity for C1 contact configurations. A line endpoint on a
  circle, together with line tangency, admits an equivalent contact equation:
  the endpoint-to-centre vector is perpendicular to the line. Retain circle
  membership and the stored sense. Arc endpoints use membership implied by
  the arc definition/intrinsic equation. Recognise structural relationships,
  never coordinate proximity; prove the equivalence and nondegeneracy
  conditions in a contact-regularization ADR in step 1.
- Every transformed equation retains its source constraints and intrinsic
  equations. Constraint-removal analysis recompiles these relationships.
  Dependence of an original tangent equation alone does not mean redundancy.
  Per-entity DoF and free directions use the regularized system. Document the
  remaining infinitesimal interpretation at unsupported singularities.
- ARCHITECTURE.md §Solve: internal system, solve and diagnose implementations;
  ordered parameters/equations, structural components (including dependencies
  whose current derivatives are zero), dense nalgebra with libm-force, LM by
  default and selectable DogLeg. No new checker dependency on these modules.
- Public API: Sketch::solve(&SolverOptions) -> Solution and
  Sketch::apply(&Solution) -> Result<(), ModelError>; SolverOptions selects
  algorithm and iteration/evaluation caps. Solution exposes coordinates,
  Status and Diagnostics, with protected source-validation information.
  Diagnostics holds total/per-entity DoF, free directions, redundant and
  conflicting sets, and degeneracy with caller ids, kinds and tags.
- apply validates source state as well as revision, coordinate completeness,
  finiteness and fixed values before any mutation; equal revision counters
  alone cannot establish compatibility. Add ModelError::Stale and typed
  application refusals. Warm start means solving after apply.
- DATA-MODEL.md §The serde form: versioned laman.solution representation,
  deterministic ordering and round-trip tests; deserialization cannot bypass
  apply validation. Public additions carry runnable rustdoc and changelog
  bullets in their introducing commits.
- Tolerance ADR in step 1: scale-relative lengths with a named absolute floor,
  dimensionless rank/angular thresholds, shared model tolerance policy but
  independent checker measurements. Numerical constants, scaling choices,
  damping/trust-region settings and caps are measured by steps 1, 3 and 4,
  not invented literals. Preserve the existing checker API where possible;
  name any necessary signature change in this plan before implementing it.
- Model-only policy API in step 2: `tolerance::{Tolerances, length,
  LENGTH_FLOOR, LENGTH_RELATIVE, RANK}`; `Tolerances::{new, at, constraint,
  entity}`: `new` freezes component scales from the input, `at` measures them
  at given coordinates for the checker (ADR-0004). These Rust support helpers are deliberately unbound;
  Python `check` adopts the policy through its existing API. `LENGTH` remains
  a compatibility constant. Compiler and LM remain internal until step 7.
- Step 7 API (module `laman::solution`): `SolverOptions {algorithm,
  max_iterations, max_evaluations, max_analysis_solves}` (non-exhaustive,
  from `Default`), `Algorithm`, `Status`, `Collapse`, `Solution
  {coordinates, status, diagnostics}` with private provenance (revision and
  a FNV-1a digest of entities, fixed set and constraints), `Diagnostics`,
  `EntityRef`/`ConstraintRef` (id, kind, tag), `Origin`, `EntityFreedom`,
  `Motion`, `Degenerate`. New `ModelError::{Stale, ForeignSolution,
  IncompleteSolution, FixedMoved}`; apply also refuses unknown ids and
  parameters, non-finite values and a negative radius, before any
  mutation, and moves the revision. Solution serde names the tangents
  `tangent_line_circle`/`tangent_circles` (no fields to tell them apart).
- Conflict ADR in step 6: bounded, deterministic analysis; inclusion-minimal
  means no member can be removed, not minimum cardinality. Numerical failure
  alone proves no conflict. Inconclusive analysis returns NotConverged;
  justified conflicts return Unsatisfiable with best effort. Establish C1
  sets analytically and with removal witnesses, including intrinsic provenance.
- ROADMAP.md M2/C1 acceptance: record the approved geometric-DoF extension and
  correct the belt's claim of first-order independent original equations.
  GLOSSARY.md defines geometric versus infinitesimal freedom and corrects its
  stale Status/Malformed wording to match the existing architecture contract.

## Acceptance

- cargo test --workspace runs fixtures 1–3 and their twins with both algorithms:
  bases/redundant twins checker-green, roadmap coordinates within each fixture's
  tolerance, DoF 0, per-entity DoF 0, no free directions, exact redundant tags.
- Conflicting twins: Unsatisfiable and exactly the roadmap conflict sets.
  Their best-effort coordinates are checked with explicit expected violations;
  base coordinates are not required of an unsatisfiable least-squares solution.
- Apply/re-solve preserves coordinate bits; isolated satisfied components retain
  their starting bits. Construction-based properties assert independently known
  DoF and checker agreement. Every discovered failure becomes a regression.
- Cover contact deletion, near-contact configurations, degeneracy, empty and
  fixed-only sketches, cap exhaustion and magnitudes near 1e±300 without panic.
  Every solve in a test is independently checked, including expected failures.
- Solution digests match native x86_64 Linux and wasm32-wasip1 locally; bless
  only checker-validated runs. Static release solves meet ≤1 ms on the named
  reference CPU. Run tools/gate.sh and tools/py-test.sh for public API changes.

## Open questions

- Settled: contact and tolerance contracts are ADR-0002/0003, measured by
  `numerical_contract.rs` in step 1. Step 2 adopts the shared model tolerance
  policy in compilation and the independent checker; step 1 changes no
  runtime API. Solver tuning/caps remain steps 3–4.
- Settled: LM uses a 0.1 geometric-tolerance polishing target for new iterates,
  preserving zero-step starts. A coupled plate regression showed that stopping
  at geometric acceptance alone can miss the unchanged coordinate bound;
  numerical defaults and measurements are in ARCHITECTURE.md §LM engine.
- Settled: the checker's length tolerance came from the sketch's guesses
  (step 2), so a far guess loosened it (regression `check-guess-scale`).
  ADR-0004: the checker measures at the checked coordinates, and solver
  acceptance is the smaller of the frozen and iterate tolerances.
- Settled: DogLeg's Gauss–Newton leg is a truncated-SVD pseudo-inverse,
  not normal equations: `JᵀJ` leaked into free directions and `JJᵀ` lost
  dependent equations (ARCHITECTURE.md §DogLeg engine). Step 5's DoF keeps
  ADR-0003's pivoted QR; the leg's cutoff is a step control, not a rank.
- Settled: a free direction lists only components above `RANK`; smaller
  ones are rounding, not motion, and become canonical +0 (ADR-0003's zero
  rule). A non-finite Jacobian row adds no rank: DoF is then an upper
  estimate, and the degeneracy is step 6's diagnostic. At the C1 bases,
  removing any one constraint frees exactly its equation count, tangents
  included, while raw nullity overcounts by 4 (belt, plate).
- Settled: conflict evidence and search are ADR-0005. Unsatisfiability is
  a local certificate (stationary least squares above tolerance,
  `STATIONARY = 1e-6`, measured between cost-comparison stalls at 3.2e-8 and
  a slow singular approach at 4.5e-6). Each member has a converged removal
  witness. A deletion filter in source order runs within 256 re-solves.
  Inconclusive is `NotConverged`. Redundancy runs in reverse source order.
- Settled (human, acceptance): the triangle twin's roadmap set {`base`,
  `left`, `right`, `apex`} was not inclusion-minimal. {`base`, `right`,
  `apex`} already conflicts: `a` lies 50 sin 60° = 43.3 > 40 from the 60°
  line through `o`. The human approved correcting
  `tests/fixtures/c1/sss-triangle-conflicting/expected.json` and ROADMAP's
  row to the three-member set; step 8's fixture test reads that file.
- No human scope decision remains: geometric DoF for C1 contacts is approved;
  general singular-sketch analysis is explicitly deferred.
